# Welcome to *Confluence: Anvil Craft*
This is a mod designed to adapt and ensure compatibility between *Confluence: Otherworld* and *AnvilCraft* game flow. its main features include:

- Lead Anvil, Mythril Anvil and Orichalcum Anvil can also be involved in functions such as Piezoelectric Crystal power generation and Anvil Processing.
- Added Lead Anvil Hammer, Mythril Anvil Hammer and Orichalcum Anvil Hammer
- Minerals such as Platinum and Hellstone can be regenerated from Mineral Fountain
- Recipe compatibility. For example, lead can replace a portion of the iron.
