A few minor changes:
- <ref item="anvilcraft:giant_anvil"/>'s bounce mode will activate Boulders surrounding it.
- <ref item="anvilcraft:fish_tank"/> no longer accepts cauldron interactions.
  - This is because of **Confluence version 1.2.6** & **Anvilcraft 1.6.0+snapshot2357**, right-clicking an <ref item="anvilcraft:fish_tank"/> with <ref item="confluence:honey_bucket"/>, <ref item="confluence:bottomless_water_bucket"/>, <ref item="confluence:bottomless_lava_bucket"/>, <ref item="confluence:bottomless_honey_bucket"/>, <ref item="confluence:bottomless_shimmer_bucket"/> or <ref item="confluence:aetherium_block"/> will cause the <ref item="anvilcraft:fish_tank"/> to turn into a <ref item="minecraft:cauldron"/>.
